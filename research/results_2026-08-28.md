# Runner Tournament Results — 2026-08-28

## Best balance: CATALYST_EARLY_RUNNER

Daily/next-session research proxy:

- Price $1–$20
- Signal-day move +5% to +15%
- RVOL 2x to <10x
- Daily anomaly rank top 25
- Fresh SEC filing marker within 3 days
- Outcome: next-session close-to-close proxy

At 0.50% round-trip friction:

| Split | n | Trades/day | Expectancy | PF |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Development | 266 | 1.06 | +1.09% | 1.39 |
| Validation | 91 | 1.11 | +1.83% | 1.77 |
| Holdout | 34 | 0.83 | +2.84% | 2.33 |
| July regime | 14 | 0.64 | +1.57% | 1.69 |

Sixteen of nineteen calendar months had positive expectancy/PF above 1 at 0.50% friction. The top five winners contributed about 11.5% of total positive gains, so the result is not driven by only a handful of trades. Pre-July bootstrap 95% expectancy intervals remained above zero, but the PF lower bound was below 1.30; probability of bootstrapped PF >1.30 was roughly 87–93% depending on trade/day versus month-block resampling.

This rule weakens materially as friction rises: at 1.00% round-trip cost PF is about 1.19 / 1.50 / 1.98 / 1.43 across the four periods. Capping every positive outcome at +20% also drops development PF below 1.30. Therefore it remains a research leader rather than an execution-ready edge.

## Robustness-first variant: CATALYST_ROBUST_3_12

Rules: $1–$20, +3% to +12% signal-day move, RVOL 2–10, >=$250k dollar volume, top-25 anomaly rank and fresh SEC filing marker within 3 days.

At 0.75% friction **and with every positive next-day return capped at +30%**:

| Split | n | Trades/day | Expectancy | PF |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Development | 164 | 0.66 | +1.00% | 1.36 |
| Validation | 55 | 0.67 | +2.73% | 2.32 |
| Holdout | 22 | 0.54 | +3.10% | 2.38 |
| July regime | 12 | 0.55 | +4.78% | 5.76 |

This is the strongest robustness-first rule found so far, but trade frequency is only about 0.5–0.7/day. Pre-July month-block bootstrap PF has a 95% interval of roughly 1.22–2.19, so the lower bound still does not clear 1.30.

## Frequency-first variant: CATALYST_HF_6_15

Rules: $1–$20, +6% to +15%, RVOL 1.5–10, >=$250k dollar volume, top-30 rank and fresh SEC filing marker within 3 days.

At 0.50% friction without winner caps it produces roughly 1.0–1.5 signals/day with PF about 1.37 / 1.61 / 2.15 / 2.63. Under the stricter 0.75% friction plus +30% winner-cap stress, PF falls to about 1.23 / 1.34 / 1.91 / 1.37. Its pre-July bootstrap PF median is about 1.30 and only about half of bootstrap samples clear PF 1.30.

**Conclusion:** no strategy found yet simultaneously clears approximately 1.5 trades/day and robust PF >=1.30 under the stricter stress assumptions.

## Rejected strategies

- `RUNNER_CONT_HF` (+5–15%, RVOL 20–50) had strong pre-July PF but July PF collapsed to roughly 0.37 with negative expectancy.
- High-volume shallow-pullback and pullback-context variants showed PF >2 in parts of history but weakened after costs and/or collapsed in July.
- Extreme fresh-filing momentum variants had attractive PF but excessive dependence on a few very large winners and were rejected from the lead group.

## Important data limitation

The `fresh_sec_3d` field means a recent SEC filing was present. It does **not** prove the filing was a positive catalyst. These are daily next-session proxies, not executable minute-level entries.
